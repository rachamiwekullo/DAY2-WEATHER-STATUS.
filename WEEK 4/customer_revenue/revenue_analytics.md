# Distributor Customer Revenue & County Concentration Analysis

A lightweight SQLite and Python (`pandas`) reporting script that links customer and order records to identify high-value repeat buyers and assess whether leading revenue accounts are concentrated in Nairobi or distributed across counties.

## Features

- **Related SQLite Tables:** Creates `customers` and `orders` tables linked by `customer_id`, with county, account tier, order details, and status.
- **Sample Distributor Data:** Seeds 12 customers and 30 orders across five counties.
- **Customer and County Analysis:** Uses an `INNER JOIN`, grouped spend totals with a `HAVING` filter, and a county-partitioned `RANK()` window function.
- **Automated Reports:** Prints all three query results using `pandas.read_sql_query`.

## Project Structure

- `revenue.py`                         # Creates the SQLite tables, seeds sample data, and runs all three queries
- `distributor_customers_orders.db`    # SQLite database generated by the script
- `revenue_analytics.md`               # Query explanations, business value, and pricing note

## What Each Query Answers & Why the Sales Manager Cares

### 1. Orders Joined with Customer Account Tier (`INNER JOIN`)

- * Lists orders alongside each customer's county and account tier (`Platinum`, `Gold`, or `Silver`), with calculated order value (`quantity * unit_price`).
- * Helps determine whether high-value orders come from priority accounts or from customers who may merit a tier upgrade, credit review, or dedicated account coverage.

### 2. Total Spend per Customer for Frequent Buyers (`GROUP BY` + `HAVING`)

-  Shows spend and order counts for customers with more than three non-cancelled orders.
-  Identifies repeat buyers the sales team may want to retain, plan stock for, or offer volume and loyalty incentives.

### 3. Top Customer per County (`CTE` + `RANK()`)

-  Ranks customers within each county by total spend and returns the top-ranked customer or customers in each county. Cancelled orders are excluded.
-  Helps assess whether top accounts are concentrated in Nairobi or include strong regional customers, informing territory coverage, delivery planning, and stock allocation.

## Recommendation

Use the frequent-buyer and top-customer reports together to prioritize account retention and regional sales coverage. Review account tiers alongside order values before changing credit limits or assigning dedicated account support.

## Findings Summary

The sample data covers 12 customers, 30 orders, and five counties. The three reports provide complementary views: order-level account context, spend among frequent buyers, and top spenders by county. The county ranking makes it possible to compare Nairobi's leading accounts with regional leaders, while the frequent-buyer query focuses on customers with more than three orders and excludes cancelled orders.

## Installation & Setup

### Prerequisites

- Python 3.7+ installed on your system.
- SQLite is included with Python; no separate database installation is required.

### Steps

Open a terminal in the project directory and install Pandas:

```powershell
python -m pip install pandas
```

## Usage

1. Open a terminal in the project directory.
2. Run the analysis script:

```powershell
python revenue.py
```

The script creates or replaces the `customers` and `orders` tables in `distributor_customers_orders.db`, inserts the bundled sample data, and prints the three reports. Running it again resets both tables to the sample data.
